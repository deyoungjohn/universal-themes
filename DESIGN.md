## Brand & Style

The design system projects an authoritative, high-stakes nocturnal environment engineered for deep analytical work, quantitative modeling, and strategic intelligence. It transforms the tactile, ink-on-parchment dignity of classical financial journalism into a low-light command surface where precision takes precedence over decorative excess.

The design philosophy unites **Minimalism** and **Tactile Editorial** tailored for dark interfaces:
- **Deep Obsidian Foundations:** Replaces harsh pure pitch black with warm, charcoal-tinted obsidian (`#12110F`) and volcanic slate (`#1A1916`), evoking heavy matte cardstock under low light rather than an uncalibrated OLED void.
- **Transitional Serif Anchors:** Deliberate, disciplined serif typography (`Newsreader`) punctuates key analytical narratives, editorial leads, and structural anchors, imbuing algorithmic readouts with institutional gravitas.
- **Muted Razor-Line Structure:** Visual architecture relies on crisp 1px borders in desaturated warm stone (`#2E2C27` and `#3E3B35`) instead of drop shadows or glowing halos.
- **Restrained Quantitative Accents:** Positive deltas, risk boundaries, and execution triggers use muted, moss-patina greens and dried-clay crimsons that register immediately without breaking chromatic discipline.

## Colors

The dark mode palette translates physical print inks into illuminated terminal values. Surfaces are built from deep earthen pitch, while typography mimics warm bone paper set against midnight stone.

- **Primary (`#F4EFE6`):** The primary typographic ink and key focus state color. Emulates natural newsprint illuminated in darkness, ensuring crisp contrast without optical vibration.
- **Secondary (`#A8A59C`):** Muted warm slate for secondary copy, supporting analytical metadata, table headers, and inactive form boundaries.
- **Tertiary (`#DDD8CD`):** High-tier interactive states, button hover fills, and emphasized statistical figures.
- **Neutral (`#12110F`):** Deep obsidian ground canvas. Anchors the viewport with low-luminance warmth that prevents eye strain during extended analysis.
- **Surfaces & Borders:** Containers layer outward from `surface-canvas` (`#12110F`) to `surface-charcoal` (`#1A1916`) and `surface-card` (`#211F1B`), separated by 1px `border-subtle` (`#2E2C27`).
- **Semantic Accents:** `accent-green` (`#3D7A4D`) and `accent-crimson` (`#B34732`) provide calibrated signals for favorable odds and risk deltas, grounded in low-opacity backing washes (`accent-green-wash` and `accent-crimson-wash`) to maintain an editorial tone.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy enforces strict analytical balance between traditional publication authority and high-density tabular calculation:

- **The Editorial 2% (`Newsreader`):** Exclusively deployed on top-level narrative displays, market overviews, and article ledes. Set in display weights with tighter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and generous vertical leading to evoke printed financial sheets.
- **The Operational 98% (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Powers application mechanics, control surfaces, data matrices, body copy, and metadata chips. Its balanced geometric proportions ensure legibility at micro scales (`0.75rem`) against dark backgrounds without optical blurring.
- **Tabular Figures & Metrics (`code-num`):** All financial numbers, probability spreads, and index tallies must mandate `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` with explicit letter tracking to guarantee absolute vertical column alignment in analytical tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is governed by a 12-column fluid grid constrained by a `1280px` maximum viewport boundary. The cadence reflects ledger sheets: compact vertical intervals paired with wide horizontal gutters.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` canvas margins. Enables asymmetric structural divisions, such as an 8-column data ledger alongside a 4-column probability monitor.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. Ancillary modules break out into full-width stacked panels beneath the main grid.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column single-column flow with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer canvas padding. Data grids compress into swipeable horizontally paged cards or vertically dense accordions.
- **Rhythm Model:** Follows an 8px base rhythm with 4px sub-intervals (`space-xs`). Component padding keeps vertical spacing disciplined (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) to preserve maximum on-screen information density.

## Elevation & Depth

In this dark system, elevation is achieved through structural luminosity tiers and razor-thin borders rather than ambient blur shadows or diffuse colored glows.

- **Tonal Stepping:** Surfaces layer upward based on operational importance:
  - **Base Canvas (`surface-canvas` / `#12110F`):** Deepest structural plane.
  - **Recessed Insets / Panels (`surface-charcoal` / `#1A1916`):** Secondary regions, table wrappers, and toolbars.
  - **Elevated Cards & Flyouts (`surface-card` / `#211F1B`):** Actionable odds modules, cards, and dialogue prompts.
  - **Hover & Active States (`surface-card-hover` / `#2A2823`):** Interactive surface highlight.
- **Low-Contrast Razor Outlines:** Every container tier is bound by a crisp 1px border (`#2E2C27`). Hovered or focused containers step up to `#3E3B35` or `#DDD8CD`.
- **Hard Offset Shadowing:** For floating popovers, dropdown menus, and sticky odds slips, blur shadows are replaced with a crisp 1px border combined with a hard, unblurred directional shadow: `0 4px 0 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The geometric envelope is restrained and disciplined (`roundedness: 1`). It preserves the structural feel of physical printing plates and technical chart displays:

- **Standard Containers & Interactive Elements:** Buttons, inputs, search inputs, and individual data cells use `0.25rem` (4px) border radiuses.
- **Cards & Contextual Dialogs:** Major module envelopes use `0.5rem` (8px) border radiuses (`rounded-lg`) to lightly soften wide sections without compromising grid alignment.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted strictly to numerical pill indicators, live market tags, and probability badges (`rounded-full`) to offer immediate geometric contrast against square ledger cells.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid warm bone `#F4EFE6` fill with near-black `#12110F` text. Weight set to `label-md`. On hover, background shifts to `#DDD8CD`; on active press, scales slightly down (98%).
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Surface fill of `#1A1916` with a 1px `#2E2C27` border and `#F4EFE6` text. Hover transitions border to `#3E3B35` and fill to `#211F1B`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, `#A8A59C` text. Hover shifts text to `#F4EFE6` with an underline decoration.

### Cards & Ledger Modules
- Constructed with `#211F1B` fill encased in a 1px `#2E2C27` outline. Header bars are separated from the card body via a single horizontal `#2E2C27` dividing rule. When interactive, cards transition background to `#2A2823` on hover with a 150ms ease curve.

### Data Tables & Odds Matrices
- Header rows feature uppercase `label-sm` text in `#A8A59C` against `#1A1916`. Body rows use horizontal 1px `#2E2C27` divider lines. Numeric values align right using `code-num` with `tabular-nums`. Row hover introduces an instant `#211F1B` wash.

### Odds Chips & Status Badges
- Encapsulated in compact `rounded-full` pills with 1px border rules. Neutral chips use `#1A1916` fill, `#2E2C27` border, and `#A8A59C` text. Positive odds deltas use `#18261C` fill, `#3D7A4D` text, and a 1px outline in `#3D7A4D`. Negative odds deltas use `#2A1714` fill, `#B34732` text, and a 1px outline in `#B34732`.

### Input Fields
- Text fields utilize `#1A1916` background, 1px `#2E2C27` border, and `body-md` typography in `#F4EFE6`. Placeholder copy is set to `#6E6B64`. Focus shifts the outline to a 1.5px solid `#F4EFE6` rule with no ambient outer glow.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- Checkboxes use 4px rounded `#1A1916` boxes bound by 1.5px `#2E2C27` strokes. Checked state fills solid with `#F4EFE6`, displaying an obsidian `#12110F` check glyph. Segmented pill tabs nest inside an `#1A1916` rail, where active tabs pop forward with `#211F1B` fill, 1px `#3E3B35` border, and `#F4EFE6` label text.
