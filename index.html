How to Apply This Theme & Wallpaper on Telegram iOS

Unlike Android, Telegram on iOS does not embed custom background photos into .tgios-theme files. Follow these simple steps on your iPhone or iPad:

1
Download Both Files

Download the .tgios-theme file and the wallpaper image.

2
Save Wallpaper to Photos

Save the downloaded image to your iPhone's Photos app.

3
Apply Theme in Telegram

Send the .tgios-theme file to your Saved Messages or AirDrop. Tap the file in chat, then tap Apply Theme.

4
Set Chat Wallpaper in Settings

In Telegram, go to Settings ⚙️ ➔ Appearance ➔ Chat Wallpaper, tap Choose from Photos, and tap Set.
