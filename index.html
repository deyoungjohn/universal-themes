How to Apply This Theme & Wallpaper on Telegram iOS

Unlike Android, Telegram on iOS does not embed custom background photos into .tgios-theme files. Follow these simple steps on your iPhone or iPad:

1
Download Both Files

Tap ⬇ Download Converted Theme to get the .tgios-theme file, and tap 🖼 Download Wallpaper Image to save the background image.

2
Save the Wallpaper to Photos

Save the downloaded image to your iPhone's Photos app (in Safari, tap the downloaded image, tap the Share icon ⎋, and select Save Image).

3
Apply the .tgios-theme File in Telegram

Send the .tgios-theme file to your Telegram (for example, to your Saved Messages chat or via AirDrop). Tap the file in chat, then tap Apply Theme.

4
Set Chat Wallpaper in Telegram Settings

In Telegram on your iPhone, navigate to:

Settings ⚙️ ➔ Appearance ➔ Chat Wallpaper

At the top, tap Choose from Photos, select your saved wallpaper image, adjust zoom or blur if desired, and tap Set.
